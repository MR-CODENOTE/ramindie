What is a Domain Name?

A domain name is your website's address on the internet (e.g., yourbusiness.com). It's what customers type to find you.

  • Makes your business look professional
  • Builds stronger branding
  • Optional but highly recommended

If you prefer not to purchase a domain, your website can still run using a co-named web address provided during deployment.

What is Hosting?

Hosting is where your website files are stored so they can be accessed online 24/7. Think of it as the "space" your website lives in.

Included in both packages:
  • Free Hosting included
  • Basic security setup
  • Secure connection

Security & SSL

SSL ensures that your website shows the secure "https" connection, protecting visitor information and making your business appear trustworthy.

SSL setup is fully included in all of our website packages.
